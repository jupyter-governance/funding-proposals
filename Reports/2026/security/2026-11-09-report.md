## Summary of Work Completed

### Communication

October was a month of communication: I gave the Jupyter security talk at the Jupyter Mini Summit of Open Source Summit Europe in Prague, wrote a blog post about security on the Jupyter blog, and attended Jupyter Open Studio at Bloomberg London where I discussed security matters informally with other maintainers. Details in the Deliverables section

### Triage

Due to focusing on communication, a large part of the triage and fixing in October was done by maintainers: [@minrk](https://github.com/minrk) published 6 JupyterHub advisories, [@krassowski](https://github.com/krassowski) handled the JupyterLab side, and [@Carreau](https://github.com/Carreau) worked with me on nbconvert reports. On my end, I handled published advisories and coordinated the release of nbconvert and jupyterlab-git, and still triaged a few.
