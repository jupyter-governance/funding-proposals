## Summary of Work Completed

September had the highest incoming volume on record: 38 new advisories were opened across the Jupyter orgs (20 in August, 22 in July). Many reports are AI-assisted and long but that is nothing new.

More than half (23) were on JupyterHub repositories, including a batch of 11 from one reporter that @minrk triaged the same day.  The JupyterHub maintainers handled most of their own reports, thanks!

I also spent a good part of my time in September on preparing for Open Source Summit in Prague and Jupyter Open Studio day in London, that I will talk about in the next report.

The follow-up funding proposal I drafted with [@Carreau](https://github.com/Carreau)  https://github.com/jupyter-governance/funding-proposals/issues/75 was approved by the Jupyter Foundation on 2026-10-02, which means **8 more hours per week will be allocated to security for the next three months (to 16h/week)**, plus some time allocated to @Careau to provide expertise and support growing the security community.

### Script used for statistics

using https://github.com/Yann-P/github-security-overview @ 0a31e9e8b6f7027780401c5ee10af3021db46418

```bash
JUPYTER_ORGS=(binder-examples binderhub-ci-repos ipython jupyter jupyter-attic jupyter-book jupyter-governance jupyter-incubator jupyter-resources jupyter-server jupyter-standard jupyter-standards jupyter-widgets jupyter-xeus jupytercon jupyterhealth jupyterhub jupyterlab jupyterlite pickleshare voila-dashboards voila-gallery)
FROM_DATE=2026-09-01
TO_DATE=2026-09-30

security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state triage | wc -l
security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state triage | datamash median 1
# Still in triage, but opened after the month
security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state triage --opened-from 2026-10-01 | wc -l
security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state triage --opened-from $FROM_DATE --opened-to $TO_DATE | wc -l
# All states, to measure incoming volume (one state at a time, see above)
for state in triage draft published closed; do
  security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state $state --opened-from $FROM_DATE --opened-to $TO_DATE
done > opened.tsv
wc -l < opened.tsv
cut -f4 opened.tsv | sort | uniq -c # by current state (column 4)

security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state draft | wc -l
security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state draft | datamash median 1
security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state draft --opened-to $(date -v-90d +%Y-%m-%d) | wc -l

security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state published | wc -l
security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state published | datamash median 3 # 3 is the column for time to publication

security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state published --published-from $FROM_DATE --published-to $TO_DATE | wc -l
security-overview "${JUPYTER_ORGS[@]}" --state published --published-from $FROM_DATE --published-to $TO_DATE | datamash median 3
```
