## Summary of Work Completed

During this period, work revolved around preparation work for Spark 4 support then actual Spark 4 support,
finishing getting Almond using its fork of its Ammonite dependency (that backs its Scala REPL support), and
getting the Almond repositories ([Almond itself](https://github.com/almond-sh/almond), [its Ammonite fork](https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite), [ammonite-spark](https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark)) ready for new releases taking
into account [Maven Central new publishing limits](https://central.sonatype.org/publish/maven-central-publishing-limits/).

ammonite-spark's build was refactored, in order to have non-regression tests for many more
Spark versions, not just a handful of them (https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/494, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/495, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/496, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/498, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/499, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/500, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/502, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/504, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/505, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/506, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/509), and ensure
support for those doesn't regress.

Actual support for Spark 4 was added later on, on top of that (https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/507).

[Almond's Ammonite fork](https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite) was put in shape for Almond's needs and for easier maintenance (https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/4, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/5, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/7, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/8, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/10, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/11), and also to limit the amount
of data being shipped during releases, to try to respect [Maven Central new publishing limits](https://central.sonatype.org/publish/maven-central-publishing-limits/) (https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/6, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/9, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/13, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/14, https://github.com/almond-sh/Ammonite/pull/15).

Almond, alongside ammonite-spark, switched to using its Ammonite fork (https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1715, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/508).

Still aiming at respecting [Maven Central new publishing limits](https://central.sonatype.org/publish/maven-central-publishing-limits/), work is on-going to limit the number of modules published by Almond
while not dropping features (https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1716, https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1721, https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1723).

Some user proposed changes were reworked (https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1710 resulting in https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1712 and https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1713), and a fix for a longstanding issue was finally merged (https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1666, fixing https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/issues/1499).
